Why Rome Is the Best City to Visit in Italy

Rome isn't just Italy's capital — it's a place where history, culture, food, and everyday life blend together in a way you won't find anywhere else. Whether you're a history lover, a foodie, or a casual holiday-maker, Rome offers something unforgettable for every type of traveler.

1. It's the “Eternal City”

Rome has been continuously inhabited for nearly 3,000 years, and you can feel that history everywhere. Walking its streets is like exploring an enormous open-air museum.

Highlights

  • The Colosseum
  • The Roman Forum
  • The Pantheon
  • Ancient roads, arches, fountains, and temples around every corner

Few places allow you to step into the past as easily as Rome.

2. The Vatican & World-Class Art

Rome is home to Vatican City — the smallest country in the world and the spiritual center of the Catholic Church. Even for visitors who aren't religious, the art and architecture are breathtaking.

Must-See Places

  • Sistine Chapel (Michelangelo's masterpiece)
  • St. Peter's Basilica
  • Vatican Museums
  • Extraordinary sculptures, frescoes, and treasures

Rome holds some of humanity's most important artistic achievements.

3. Unbeatable Food

Rome's cuisine is simple, bold, and delicious — trying authentic Roman dishes is a highlight of any visit.

Famous Roman Foods

  • Carbonara
  • Cacio e Pepe
  • Amatriciana
  • Supplì (fried rice balls with mozzarella)
  • Fresh Roman-style pizza
  • Excellent gelato everywhere

Whether you dine in a cosy trattoria or grab street food, Rome's food culture is unforgettable.

4. Iconic Landmarks Everywhere

Rome has so many world-famous sites that you can see multiple landmarks just by walking.

Other Favorites

  • Trevi Fountain
  • Spanish Steps
  • Castel Sant'Angelo
  • Piazza Navona
  • Trastevere (a lively, charming neighborhood perfect for evening walks)

Even a simple stroll becomes a sightseeing tour.

5. Perfect Mix of Ancient and Modern

Rome is one of the few cities where you can admire a 2,000-year-old temple, enjoy a modern espresso, and shop for Italian fashion — all within minutes.

The City Blends

  • Historic ruins
  • Renaissance palaces
  • Baroque churches
  • Modern nightlife
  • Stylish boutiques

Rome is a living city, not just a historical one.

6. Easy to Explore

Rome is very walkable, and when needed, it has multiple transportation options:

  • Metro lines
  • Buses
  • Trams
  • Hop-on-hop-off sightseeing buses

Many visitors end up walking simply because every street feels like a discovery.

7. Amazing Day Trips

Rome makes an excellent base for exploring other iconic locations.

  • Pompeii & Naples (train)
  • Florence (about 90 minutes by high-speed train)
  • Ostia Antica (ancient port city)
  • Tivoli (Villa d'Este & Hadrian's Villa)
  • Castelli Romani (beautiful hill towns and wine areas)

8. Vibrant Atmosphere

Rome has an energy unlike anywhere else — lively piazzas, street artists, fountains, music, and locals enjoying life outdoors. Evening strolls (passeggiata) are a beloved tradition.

Rome's atmosphere blends:

  • Culture
  • Warmth
  • Chaos
  • Beauty
  • Tradition

This mix is part of what makes the city unforgettable.

Final Reason: Rome Has Something for Everyone

  • History lovers enjoy ruins and museums
  • Food lovers adore the cuisine
  • Families enjoy parks, gelato, and sightseeing
  • Couples find the city romantic
  • Solo travelers appreciate walkability and culture
  • Shoppers love markets and boutiques

No matter who you are, Rome delivers a holiday you'll never forget.
